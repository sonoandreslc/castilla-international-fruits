## Brand & Style

The brand occupies the intersection between Colombian agricultural heritage and the ultra-premium hospitality, retail, and culinary standards of the United Arab Emirates. The design aesthetic balances organic vitality with sovereign Middle Eastern luxury: disciplined, architectural, and tactile.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Editorial Luxury** with **Precision B2B Utility**. Interfaces avoid rustic tropes, relying instead on razor-sharp typography, generous spatial architecture, and subtle champagne gold flourishes against deep forest surfaces. The visual language conveys peerless cold-chain rigor, phytosanitary mastery, and elite status to executive procurement directors, luxury hotel chefs, and sovereign distributors across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and the wider GCC.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, high-contrast atmosphere calibrated for international trade:

- **Primary (`#0D472B`) — Imperial Emerald:** Anchors key navigation frameworks, premium hero sections, and corporate validation seals. It represents fertile high-altitude Colombian soil and ecological stewardship.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27`) — Champagne Gold:** Used for sovereign accents, verification crests, Halal badges, active states, and focal metrics. A supporting lighter gold tone (`#E5C158`) serves as highlight states.
- **Tertiary (`#0F5132`) — Andean Forest:** Supplies tonal depth for secondary surfaces, container fills, and hover variants of primary interactive elements.
- **Neutral (`#111827`) — Onyx Charcoal:** Provides high-legibility typographic contrast across light surfaces, metadata panels, and legal disclaimers.
- **Surface Foundations:** Crisp porcelain whites (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8F9FA`) define daylight views, bounded by subtle slate rules (`#E2E8F0`) to maintain tabular clarity in trade ledgers and specification sheets.

## Typography

Typographic scale balances geometric presence with operational efficiency. Headings set in **Montserrat** bring architectural structure and luxury weight, rendered in semi-bold cuts with tight negative tracking for monumental presentation.

Body copy and interface controls leverage **Inter** for clean glyph definition, high legibility across multilingual numeric tables (Brix levels, cargo weights, freight schedules, HS codes), and seamless pairing of Arabic numerals alongside AED/USD currency values. All uppercase metadata elements utilize expanded letter-spacing to reinforce precision and prestige.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display both immersive editorial product spreads and data-dense export manifests:

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid with a maximum content width of `1440px`, centered with `margin: 3rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Complex quoting matrices reflow into stacked segment cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column architecture with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

Vertical rhythm relies on multiples of an 8px base unit. Micro-spaces (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) separate tightly coupled values like unit prices and HS codes, while macro-spaces (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) isolate institutional credibility markers, farm provenance maps, and quotation modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **low-contrast architectural outlines** complemented by **tinted ambient shadows** to convey high-end physical weight:

- **Level 0 (Flat/Base):** Pure `#FFFFFF` and porcelain `#F8F9FA` surfaces bounded by precise 1px slate borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Cards & Data Tables):** Elevated via `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(13, 71, 43, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.06)`, maintaining a hairline border in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Menus & Quotation Drawers):** `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(13, 71, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)` with a delicate champagne border accent (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.3)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sovereign Alerts):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.25)` overlaying a backdropped forest veil (`rgba(13, 71, 43, 0.65)` with an `8px` blur).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** corner treatment (`0.25rem` / 4px base radius; `0.5rem` / 8px for containers; `0.75rem` / 12px for modal overlays). This geometric restraint reinforces executive seriousness, cold-chain precision, and logistical discipline. Pill shapes are reserved exclusively for status chips, compliance seals, and currency toggle indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign:** Solid Emerald (`#0D472B`) fill, white text, 4px corner radius, inset hairline border of gold shimmer on hover (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Luxury:** Translucent champagne gold background (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.1)`), borders in `#C59B27`, text in `#0D472B`.
- **Tertiary Minimal:** Borderless text link in `#0D472B` with an animated 1px underline in `#C59B27`.

### Cards & Catalogue Panels
- **Produce Showcase Card:** Built with `#FFFFFF` background, subtle `#E2E8F0` border, and 8px radius. Product imagery showcases pristine tropical produce (e.g., Golden Passion Fruit, Hass Avocado, Physalis) with micro-badges for Brix degree levels and GlobalG.A.P. certification.
- **Cold-Chain Assurance Card:** Dark emerald background (`#0D472B`) with soft gold typography, gold divider lines, and metrics detailing Bogota-to-Dubai transit times, controlled atmosphere specs, and phytosanitary guarantees.

### Compliance Seals & Badges
- **Halal & G.A.P. Certifications:** Emblems bordered with a 1px `#C59B27` stroke, subtle champagne background fill (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.08)`), pairing an emerald checkmark icon with crisp, tracked uppercase typography.
- **Cargo Status Chips:** Micro pill containers (`rounded-full`) denoting lot availability (e.g., "Air Cargo Ready", "Pre-Order Harvest", "Dubai Stock Arrived") using semantic emerald, amber, and gold indicators.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Calculators
- **Inputs:** Clean `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with `#E2E8F0` borders, shifting to a solid 1px `#0D472B` and a subtle 2px champagne ring (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.2)`) on active focus.
- **B2B Bulk Quotation Matrix:** Interactive step-form allowing procurement directors to calculate pallet quantities, maritime reefer vs. air freight configurations, and live pricing conversions.

### Currency & Locale Switcher
- **Dual-State Capsule:** Segmented toggle allowing instant selection between **USD ($)** and **AED (د.إ)**, alongside EN / AR language alternatives, accented with `#0D472B` active tabs and gold indicator dots.
